Project: Muse Privacy Policy
《缪斯计划》隐私政策

Developer: Rinzz Co. Ltd. · Based on the Rinzz Game privacy policy last updated April 18, 2022.

This policy describes how Rinzz Co. Ltd. handles personal information in connection with Project: Muse and its related services. Please read it before using the game. For questions or privacy requests, contact work@rinzz.com.

English

1. Scope

This policy covers information collected when you use Project: Muse and its related game services. It does not govern independent websites linked from the game or this website; consult those sites' own privacy policies.

2. Information collection and use

To provide interactive game services, we may ask you to provide personal information. When you contact us or use interactive features such as surveys, we may retain the name, email address, contact details and time you provide. Our servers may record technical and usage information such as the IP address of your connecting device, time of use, browser information and the pages or services selected. We use this information to provide, maintain and improve game services and to respond to you.

We may analyze survey results and publish aggregated statistics or explanatory material for research and service improvement. Such publications do not identify individual users.

3. Protection of information

We use technical and organizational safeguards for personal information and restrict access to authorized personnel and service providers who need it to provide the service and are subject to confidentiality obligations.

4. External links

Links to third-party websites may appear in the game or on this website. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this one.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers working on our behalf under confidentiality obligations, with your consent, where required by law or legal obligations, to protect users and the public, or to prevent fraud and other misuse of the service. Statistical or research disclosures should not identify individual users.

6. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the services or legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page or otherwise presented in the game. For questions about personal information or to request assistance, contact work@rinzz.com.

简体中文

一、适用范围

本政策说明 Rinzz Co. Ltd. 如何处理您使用《缪斯计划》及相关游戏服务时的个人信息。本游戏或网站链接到的独立第三方网站适用其各自的隐私政策。

二、信息收集与使用

为提供互动游戏服务，我们可能需要您提供个人信息。您通过电子邮件或问卷等互动功能联系我们时，我们可能保留您提供的姓名、电子邮箱、联系信息和使用时间。服务器也可能记录连接设备的 IP 地址、使用时间、浏览器信息和访问或选择的服务等技术与使用信息，用于提供、维护和改进服务，以及回复您的问题。

我们可能对问卷和服务使用情况进行统计分析，并发布不涉及具体个人身份的汇总统计信息或说明。

三、信息保护

我们采取技术和组织层面的保护措施。仅授权人员及为提供服务所必需的合作方能够按保密要求接触相关个人信息。

四、外部链接

游戏或本网站可能提供第三方网站链接。第三方网站的信息处理方式以其自身隐私政策为准。

五、与第三方共享

我们不会出售或出租您的个人信息。为提供服务而受托的合作方须履行保密义务；此外，在您同意、法律或合同要求、保护用户或公共利益、防范欺诈及其他滥用行为等情况下，可能共享必要信息。统计或研究披露不应识别具体个人。

六、政策更新与联系

我们可能因服务或法律要求变化更新本政策，并在本页面或游戏中公布。有关个人信息的问题或协助请求，请联系 work@rinzz.com。
